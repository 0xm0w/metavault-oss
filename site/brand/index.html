Visual Identity System

MetaVault OSS Brand Guidelines

A unified visual system representing non-possessory security. The logo marries the continuous, infinite ribbon curves of Meta with the indestructible fortress geometry of the Vault. Built around high-contrast teal, deep void blacks, crisp white, and hairline slate.

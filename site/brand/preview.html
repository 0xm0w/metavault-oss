Anatomy

  • Vault body — rounded frame (368×352u, r88) on two feet; the open interior is the "window" into the vault.
  • Hinges — two accent capsules that cut through the frame with a clear gap, so they survive in mono and at small sizes.
  • AI chip — square die with 3 pins a side, pin-1 marker, and a geometric AI monogram knocked out of the die (no font dependency).
  • Edit the numbers in TIERS inside scripts/build_brand.py, then run python scripts/build_brand.py. Every file below is regenerated.
